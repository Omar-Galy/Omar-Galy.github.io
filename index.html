Work

Galy Fashion2026

A demo men's clothing shop that runs entirely inside WhatsApp. You can ask about products and get their photos, pick a size and colour, and place, change or cancel an order.

The bot never saves anything by itself. It shows you the order, you press a confirm button, and only then is it written down. That way it can't tell you an order went through when it didn't.

It's a demo, so nothing is actually sold or shipped.
