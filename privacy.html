Privacy policy

Last updated 21 September 2026

This policy covers the Galy Fashion demo chatbot on WhatsApp, run by Omar Galy (Galy AI), Mansoura, Egypt. Galy Fashion is a demonstration shop: nothing is sold or shipped and no payment is taken.

Please don't send real personal details. Because this is a demo, you can use a made-up name, address and account number when you try an order or a return.

What is collected

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and profile name, which WhatsApp sends with every message.
  • The messages you send and the replies you receive, kept as a conversation log.
  • What you type while trying the demo: for an order, a name, phone number, city and address; for a return, the refund details you enter.

Why

Only to run the conversation and the demo order flow. Your data is not sold, not used for advertising, and the bot never messages you first.

Who handles it

  • WhatsApp (Meta) delivers the messages.
  • Supabase stores the conversation and the demo orders.
  • An AI model, reached through OpenRouter, receives the text of the conversation in order to write each reply.

How long it is kept

Until you ask for it to be deleted, or until the demo is shut down.

Your choices

Email galy.automation@gmail.com from any address, with the WhatsApp number you used, to get a copy of your data or have it deleted.

بنجمع إيه

  • رقم واتساب بتاعك واسم البروفايل، واللي واتساب بيبعتهم مع كل رسالة.
  • الرسايل اللي بتبعتها والردود اللي بتوصلك، ومتسجلة كسجل للمحادثة.
  • اللي بتكتبه وانت بتجرب: في الطلب اسم ورقم تليفون ومدينة وعنوان، وفي الاسترجاع بيانات رد الفلوس اللي بتدخلها.

ليه

مين بيتعامل معاها

  • واتساب (Meta) بيوصّل الرسايل.
  • Supabase بيخزن المحادثة وطلبات التجربة.
  • نموذج ذكاء اصطناعي عن طريق OpenRouter بيستلم نص المحادثة عشان يكتب كل رد.
